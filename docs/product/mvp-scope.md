# MVP Scope

The first MVP is successful when all six mathematical levels are playable, understandable, and mathematically correct from a level-selection interface.

## In Scope

- A level-selection interface.
- Six playable mathematical puzzle levels.
- Basic story progression and context.
- A 2D pixel-art presentation.
- Placeholder assets during development.
- Mouse and touch interaction through a shared pointer model.
- Win, loss, retry, and return-to-selection loops.
- Mathematically correct NPC and bot behavior.

## Out of Scope for the Initial MVP

- An explorable overworld.
- Platforming or complex character movement.
- An inventory system.
- A large dialogue system.
- A sophisticated save or progression system unless it later proves necessary.
- Final production art.
- Elaborate animations.
- Advanced accessibility or hint systems.
- Unnecessary generic puzzle-framework architecture.

Scope may expand after the complete six-level MVP works. Until then, additions should support delivery or validation of those six levels.
