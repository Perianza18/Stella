# Story and Level Index

This document is the authoritative narrative sequence for Stella's six-level MVP.

## Base Story

Stella is an astronaut otter.

Like all otters, she keeps her favorite rock in a pouch of skin beneath her arm—the same rock she has carried since she was little.

During a routine mission, her ship crosses an intergalactic anomaly and the rock disappears.

It does not fall. It is not stolen. It simply stops being there.

Stella does not know whether it was destroyed or transported somewhere else in the universe, but she refuses to stop searching. She reprograms her ship as an explorer and sets off.

## Level 1 — Double Lock

Stella retraces her ship's route before the anomaly. The records point to an abandoned space station where an old information broker named Zyx-7 archived information about similar anomalies.

If Stella can open the sealed archive, she may discover where to search next.

## Level 2 — The Ruins Board

The archives lead Stella to a planet covered in ruins from an extinct civilization.

An alien nomad—the last keeper of that civilization's memory—claims to have seen objects appear and disappear through anomalies similar to Stella's. The nomad knows someone who may know more.

Before sharing that information, however, the nomad demands that Stella play the sacred game of the ancient builders.

## Level 3 — The Collector

The nomad sends Stella to The Vault, the largest collection of rare objects in the known universe.

The Collector has catalogued countless objects lost or recovered across space, including objects associated with anomalies. He may know what happened to Stella's rock. However, he refuses to share information unless Stella defeats him in his gravitational duel.

The Collector does not possess Stella's rock, and the duel uses a neutral holographic game token. After Stella succeeds, the Collector provides a clue about the anomaly signature and Krik territory.

## Level 4 — The Krik Greeting

The Collector recognizes the anomaly signature. Similar anomalies occur near the outer corridor, inside Krik territory.

To cross the territory, Stella must participate in the mandatory Krik greeting ritual. The Krik do not make exceptions.

## Level 5 — The 200 Gems

The Krik confirm that objects from Stella's anomaly have been observed in the Vrex sector.

On Vrex, an ancient Guardian records objects and events passing through that region of space. If anyone saw Stella's rock, it was likely the Guardian. The Guardian only shares information with someone who defeats him in the Game of 200 Gems.

## Level 6 — Galactic Customs

The Guardian provides exact coordinates. Stella's rock lies inside a restricted border sector, but a customs satellite blocks the route.

Agent Glip requires Stella to solve a smuggling problem before granting authorization. Stella solves it. Customs opens.

On the other side is Stella's rock, exactly where the anomaly left it.
