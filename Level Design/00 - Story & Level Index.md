# Base Story

Stella is an astronaut otter. Like all otters, she keeps her favorite rock in a pouch of skin under her arm — the same one since she was little. During a routine mission, her ship crosses an intergalactic anomaly and the rock disappears. It doesn't fall, it isn't stolen — it simply stops existing. Stella doesn't know if it was destroyed or if it's somewhere else in the universe. But she can't just stop looking for it. She reprograms her ship as an explorer and sets off.
